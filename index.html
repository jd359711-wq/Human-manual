The Prompt Library: all 106 prompts

Tap a topic, then Copy any prompt and paste it into your AI tool. Replace anything in [BRACKETS] with your own details, and leave out anything on the Chapter 32 list.

  1. Add one sentence of background. "I'm a beginner" or "I have 20 minutes" changes the answer a lot.
  2. Say what to leave out. "Don't add facts I didn't give you."
  3. Check the answer. Every prompt makes a draft. You decide what's true and what gets sent.
Home and Family 12
  1. Plan five weeknight dinners for [number] people using mostly [ingredients you have]. Each should take under [minutes] minutes. Then give me one combined shopping list grouped by store section.
  2. My [child’s age]-year-old asked me “[their question].” Help me explain it in a way they’ll understand, with one example from everyday life.
  3. Make a weekly chore chart for [names and ages]. Keep it fair, rotate the unpopular jobs and fit it on one page.
  4. Help me plan a [birthday, reunion, holiday] for [number] people on a budget of [$amount]. Give me a timeline starting [weeks] weeks before.
  5. Turn this recipe into one for [number] servings. Show the new amount of every ingredient: [paste recipe]
  6. I have [ingredient] that needs to be used up today. Suggest three ways to use it that don’t require a trip to the store.
  7. Write a friendly note to my neighbor about [issue, like a fence, noise or a shared driveway]. Keep it warm, specific and under 100 words.
  8. Help me make a packing list for [number] days in [place] in [month], for [who’s going]. Group it by category.
  9. Give me a 15-minute cleaning plan for [room] that I can repeat every day this week.
  10. Suggest five screen-free activities for a rainy afternoon with kids ages [ages], using things most homes already have.
  11. Help me write a thank-you note to [person] for [what they did]. Mention [one specific detail] and keep it sincere, not flowery.
  12. My [appliance] is [describe the problem]. What are the most common causes, which ones can I safely check myself, and when should I call a professional?
Money and Bills 10
  1. Explain this bill line by line in plain English. Then list questions I should ask the company: [paste the bill with account numbers replaced by [ACCOUNT]]
  2. Help me build a simple monthly budget. My take-home pay is [$amount]. My fixed bills are [list]. Show where the rest goes and do the math step by step.
  3. Compare these two options for me in a table: [option A] versus [option B]. Include the total cost over [time period]. Show your math.
  4. What questions should I ask before signing up for [subscription, phone plan, gym, service]? Include questions about cancellation.
  5. Write a polite message asking [company] to [lower my bill, waive a fee, cancel a service]. Mention that I’ve been a customer for [time].
  6. Explain [a money term, like APR, escrow or a deductible] in plain English, with a small example using round numbers.
  7. List every subscription a typical household forgets it’s paying for, so I can check my statements for them.
  8. I want to save [$amount] by [date]. Show me how much that is per week and per month, and suggest three small cuts that would get me there.
  9. Help me organize my paperwork for [taxes, a loan application, a move]. Give me a checklist of documents people usually need. Don’t give me tax advice.
  10. Here’s an offer I received: [paste it]. What are the warning signs that it could be a scam? What should I check before responding?
Health and Appointments (Organize, Don’t Diagnose) 8
  1. I have an appointment about [topic]. Help me write a short list of clear questions to ask. Don’t give me medical advice.
  2. Help me make a simple log to track [symptom, sleep, meals] for two weeks so I can show my doctor. Make it a table I can print.
  3. Explain what [a medical term from a letter or report] generally means in plain English. Then tell me what questions to ask my doctor about it.
  4. Make a one-page list of my medications and doses to carry in my wallet. Here they are: [list]. Format it clearly, and remind me to double-check it against the labels.
  5. Help me prepare to talk with [a family member] about [a health decision or caregiving plan]. Suggest a calm way to start the conversation.
  6. Suggest a gentle 10-minute stretching routine for someone who [sits all day, is just starting out]. Remind me to check with my doctor if anything hurts.
  7. What questions should I ask when choosing [a new doctor, a physical therapist, a care facility]?
  8. Turn these notes from my appointment into a clear summary I can share with my family: [notes]. Don’t add anything the doctor didn’t say.
Work and Email 12
  1. Rewrite this email so it’s clearer and shorter. Keep every fact and date the same: [paste email]
  2. Help me reply to this message. I want to say [your point] politely but firmly: [paste message]
  3. Turn these meeting notes into decisions, action items with owners and open questions. If an owner or date is missing, mark it “missing” instead of guessing: [notes]
  4. I need to ask my manager for [time off, a raise, help, a change]. Help me plan what to say in three short points.
  5. Summarize this long document in five bullet points, then list anything that looks like a deadline or a commitment: [paste document]
  6. Help me write a short, friendly follow-up to someone who hasn’t replied in [time]. No guilt-tripping.
  7. Make a checklist from this process so a new coworker could follow it: [describe the process]
  8. Give me three subject lines for this email, each under eight words: [paste email]
  9. I have [list of tasks] and [hours] hours. Help me put them in order and tell me what can wait until tomorrow.
  10. Rewrite this paragraph so a customer with no technical background would understand it: [paste paragraph]
  11. Help me prepare for a meeting about [topic]. What questions are people likely to ask, and how could I answer briefly?
  12. Draft a polite “no” to [request]. Offer [an alternative] if I want to.
Running a Small Business (Rosa’s Shelf) 12
  1. Write three versions of a social media post announcing [news], for [Facebook, Instagram, a sign in the window]. Keep each one under 50 words.
  2. Turn this rough list into a clear, friendly price list for customers: [list]
  3. Draft a reply to this customer review. Thank them, address [the specific complaint] and invite them back. Don’t make promises I didn’t list: [paste review]
  4. Write a simple refund policy for a [type of business], in plain English, that covers [situations]. Mark anything I should check with a professional.
  5. Suggest ten names for [a new product, a special, an event]. Make them short, friendly and easy to say out loud.
  6. Help me write a job post for a part-time [role]. Include duties, hours and what makes this a good place to work.
  7. Turn these customer questions I get all the time into a short FAQ for my website: [list questions and your answers]
  8. I sold [numbers] last month and [numbers] this month. Help me see what changed and what questions to ask. Show the math.
  9. Write a short, warm email to regular customers about [change, closure, new hours].
  10. Help me plan a simple promotion for a slow [day or month] that costs under [$amount].
  11. Create a daily opening and closing checklist for [type of business].
  12. Rewrite my business description so it sounds friendly and clear, under 80 words: [paste description]
School and Learning (Dev’s Shelf) 10
  1. Explain [topic] step by step, then ask me three questions to check that I understood. Wait for my answers.
  2. Quiz me on [topic], one question at a time. Give a hint before the answer if I get it wrong.
  3. Here’s my explanation of [topic]. What did I get right, what did I get wrong, and what did I leave out? [your explanation]
  4. Make a study plan for [test] on [date]. I can study [minutes] a day. Mix review and practice.
  5. Give me five practice problems on [topic], from easy to hard. Don’t show the answers until I ask.
  6. Explain the difference between [two things people mix up], with one example of each.
  7. I don’t understand this paragraph from my textbook. Explain it in simpler words without changing the meaning: [paste paragraph]
  8. Help me brainstorm ideas for a [essay, project, science fair] about [topic]. Just ideas. I’ll do the writing.
  9. Make a set of flashcards for [topic]: term on one side, short definition on the other.
  10. What are common mistakes students make with [topic], and how can I avoid them?
Finding a Job 8
  1. Here’s my experience: [list what you actually did]. Here’s a job post: [paste it]. Which of my experience fits best, and how should I describe it? Don’t add skills I didn’t list.
  2. Turn these job duties into strong résumé bullet points that start with action words. Keep them truthful: [list duties]
  3. Write a short cover letter for [job] using only these facts about me: [facts]
  4. Ask me the ten most common interview questions for [job], one at a time. After each answer, give me one tip to improve it.
  5. Help me explain [a gap in my work history, a career change] honestly and briefly in an interview.
  6. Write a polite follow-up email after an interview for [job] with [interviewer’s role].
  7. What questions should I ask the interviewer at the end of an interview for [job]?
  8. Compare these two job offers in a table: [details]. Include pay, benefits, commute and anything else I list.
Travel and Getting Around 6
  1. Plan a [number]-day trip to [place] for [who’s going] who like [interests], on a budget of [$amount]. Mark anything I should confirm, like hours and prices.
  2. What should I know before visiting [place] in [month], including weather, customs and common tourist mistakes?
  3. Help me compare [driving, flying, train] for a trip from [place] to [place]. Include time, rough cost and hassle.
  4. Translate these five phrases into [language] and show me how to pronounce them: [phrases]
  5. My flight was [delayed, canceled]. What should I ask the airline, and what information should I write down?
  6. Make a checklist of things to do before leaving home for [number] days.
Tech Help 8
  1. Explain step by step how to [task] on a [phone or computer model]. Ask me which version I have if it matters.
  2. My [device or app] is [problem]. List the simplest fixes to try first, in order.
  3. What does this error message mean in plain English, and what should I try? [paste message]
  4. Help me pick a strong, memorable password method. Don’t create an actual password for me.
  5. I got this message: [paste it with personal details removed]. Does it look like a scam? What should I check?
  6. Explain what [a tech term] means and whether I need to care about it.
  7. Walk me through backing up the photos on my [phone] so I don’t lose them.
  8. Help me set up [a feature] for my [parent, grandparent] so it’s as simple as possible for them to use.
Writing and Creativity 8
  1. Write a short poem for [occasion] for [person], mentioning [details]. Make it [funny, sweet, simple].
  2. Help me write a toast for [event] that’s about one minute long. Here are some memories: [list]
  3. I’m writing a story about [idea]. Ask me five questions that would help me figure out what happens next.
  4. Make this paragraph sound more like spoken conversation and less formal: [paste paragraph]
  5. Give me ten writing prompts about [theme] to get me started.
  6. Help me write a short bio for [purpose] using these facts: [facts]. Keep it under 60 words.
  7. Read this and tell me the one change that would improve it most: [paste writing]
  8. Help me plan a family history project. What questions should I ask older relatives?
Checking an AI Answer 6
  1. Before I use this answer, list every factual claim in it that I should verify, and say what kind of source would confirm each one: [paste answer]
  2. What might be wrong or missing in your last answer? Be specific.
  3. Show your math step by step so I can check it with a calculator.
  4. Which parts of your answer are general knowledge, and which parts would change by date, location or product?
  5. Rewrite your answer, keeping only what comes from the document I gave you. Mark anything you can’t find in it.
  6. Give me the strongest argument against what you just said.
Getting Unstuck 6
  1. You keep [describe the problem]. Here’s exactly what I want instead: [description].
  2. Let’s start over. Here’s a short summary of what I need: goal, facts, rules and the format I want back. [summary]
  3. Ask me up to five questions that would help you do this job better. Then wait for my answers.
  4. Give me three different approaches to this, and tell me what each one is best for.
  5. Make this shorter. Cut it by half without losing any facts.
  6. That’s close. Keep everything the same except [the one thing to change].

The Workflow Lab (Chapters 28–29)

human_manual_workflow_lab.zip
5 files · about 6 KB · Python 3.9 or newer · runs offline
Download the lab

Extract the ZIP, open a terminal in that folder and run:

python3 workflow_lab.py
python3 -m unittest test_workflow_lab.py
python3 capture_examples.py

On Windows, type py instead of python3. The lab uses only Python's standard library and a local SQLite file. It doesn't call an AI service, connect an inbox or send real email. It's a good habit to read code before you run it, including code from a book: open the files after you unzip them and read them first.
